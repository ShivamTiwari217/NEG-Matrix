Sources and assumptions
  • Grid carbon intensity (2025): Ember, Global Electricity Review 2026. China 525, US 384, EU 210, India 670, world 458 gCO₂e/kWh.
  • Cell/battery production footprint: secondary summary of regional LCA estimates (EU 57–69, US 67, China 87–96 kgCO₂e/kWh); Peiseler et al. 2024, Nat. Commun. 15:10301 gives global NMC811 median 74. Greenpeace reports 99–136 for NMC cells in China and 52 (France) to 126.5 (Germany). Check the primary papers before citing.
  • India's embodied footprint is an assumption: set equal to China-made cells because nearly all of India's Li-ion demand is imported.
  • Degradation: Geotab 2025, 22,700 EVs: 2.3%/yr average; hot climates +0.4%/yr. India uses 2.7%, which assumes a hot-climate fleet.
  • Carbon price: EPA 2023 social cost of carbon, $190/tCO₂ (2020 USD, 2% discount rate), per RFF. Contested and adjustable. It scales dollar values only; the sign of NEG, the zones and g/l do not depend on it.
  • Recycling credit defaults to 0%. Literature spans 13–17% (ICCT) to 39% (Stegemann & Gutsch review), and I found no consistent regional recovery-rate data to hardcode a per-region credit.
  • US states: EPA, eGRID Summary Tables 2023 (rev. 2, Jun 2025), Table 3, CO₂e total output emission rate by state. 2023 data, generation-based, direct combustion only.
  • EU countries: Ember carbon intensity per country (2025 or latest), as listed on Projet Celsius citing Ember's Global Electricity Review 2026 and Our World in Data (download the CSV there to verify). Rankings cross-checked loosely against the EEA indicator (direct emissions; its values are in an interactive chart I could not read).
  • India states: Ember, India Electricity Data, yearly long-format CSV (file india_yearly_full_release_long_format.csv), variable “CO2 intensity” and “Total Generation”, 2024, 36 states/UTs; methodology PDF at files.ember-energy.org. RE share of procurement (FY2024) from Ember–IEEFA SET 2026.
  • Do not mix India factors: Ember's 670 g/kWh (2025, lifecycle) and CEA's 727 g/kWh (cited in Ember's EV report from CEA's CO₂ Baseline Database v20) differ in year and method.
  • Map geometry: US, Census Bureau cartographic boundaries via us-atlas (ISC); EU, Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain); India, district polygons from udit-001/india-maps-data dissolved to states. All simplified for display. The India repository carries no licence and says it curates public data, so check provenance before publishing; DataMeet (CC BY 4.0) is the licensed alternative but treats Ladakh as part of J&K.
  • From the paper: d₁ = 0.20, d₂ = 0.70 (BDL at second-life and end-of-life transitions); BMS life extension of 15–60% in the literature, up to ~40% per the Lithion Power cofounder, hence θ = 40%.
  • Assumed: 60 kWh pack; 3,000 kWh/yr charging energy per battery; second-life degradation rate 2.5× first-life (b₂ = 0.4·b₁; the paper only requires b₂ < b₁). The paper's Appendix B.5 parameter table was not in the PDF I have.
